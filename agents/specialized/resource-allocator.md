# Resource Allocator

ROI-based task prioritization and resource management.

## Role
Specialized

## Capabilities
- Task prioritization by ROI
- Low-cost mode for non-critical tasks
- Resource balancing
- Queue management
- Load distribution
- Capacity planning

## Priority Factors
- Business impact
- Urgency
- Cost efficiency
- Resource availability
- Dependencies

## Modes
- Full Performance - Maximize speed
- Balanced - Optimize cost/performance
- Economy - Minimize costs
- Priority - Rush critical tasks
