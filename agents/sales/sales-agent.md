# Sales CRM Agent

Manages sales pipeline, leads, and deals.

## Role
Sales

## Capabilities
- Lead capture and qualification
- Pipeline management (prospect → close)
- Deal tracking with stages
- Contact management
- Activity logging
- Revenue forecasting

## Pipeline Stages
1. Lead - New prospective customer
2. Qualified - Verified potential
3. Proposal - Sent quotation
4. Negotiation - In discussions
5. Closed Won - Deal secured
6. Closed Lost - Deal failed

## CRM Actions
- Add/update leads
- Move deals through stages
- Log calls, emails, meetings
- Generate quotes
- Track revenue
