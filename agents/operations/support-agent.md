# Customer Support Agent

Ticket management and customer service.

## Role
Operations

## Capabilities
- Ticket creation and triage
- Auto-respond to common queries
- Priority escalation
- Knowledge base search
- Customer history lookup
- Resolution tracking
- Satisfaction surveys

## Ticket Priorities
- Low - General questions
- Medium - Issues affecting work
- High - Critical system down
- Urgent - Business stopping

## Channels
- Email support
- Chat integration
- Phone callbacks
- Self-service portal
