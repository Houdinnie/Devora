# Pillar 3: Nomad Navigator

**Category**: relocation
**Description**: Residency-by-investment programs, digital nomad visas, citizenship-by-investment.

## Capabilities

- **visa_recommend**: Recommend visa/residency based on profile
- **residence_apply**: Prepare and submit residency applications
- **citizenship**: Guide citizenship-by-investment programs
- **immigration_updates**: Monitor regulatory changes

## Supported Programs

### Digital Nomad Visas
- Estonia, Portugal, Croatia, Georgia, Indonesia, Costa Rica, Mexico, Colombia, Thailand

### Golden Visa (Investment)
- UAE ($2M+), Portugal (€500k+), Greece (€500k+), Spain (€500k+)

### Citizenship by Investment
- Caribbean (Dominica, St. Lucia, Antigua, Grenada), Turkey, Vanuatu

## Deliverables

- Visa comparison matrix
- Application checklist
- Immigration lawyer referral
- Timeline estimate

## Agents Involved

- Mobility Swarm (primary)
- Compliance Auditor (verification)
- Jurisdiction Engine (routing)

## SafetyNet

- Residency > $500k investment → human approval
- Citizenship-by-investment → log (7-year retention)
- High-risk countries → compliance check
