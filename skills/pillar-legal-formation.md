# Pillar 2: Legal Formation

**Category**: legal
**Description**: Company registration, IBC structures, LLC formation, and nominee director services.

## Capabilities

- **structure_recommend**: Recommend optimal entity structure based on user goals
- **formation_file**: Prepare and file formation documents
- **nominee_services**: Provide nominee directors/shareholders where allowed
- **ongoing_compliance**: Annual filings, registered agent services
