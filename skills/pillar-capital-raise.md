# Pillar 9: Capital Raise

**Category**: finance
**Description**: Investor introductions, pitch deck optimization, due diligence preparation.

## Capabilities

- **pitch_deck**: Generate pitch deck, one-pager, pitch script
- **investor_match**: Identify investors by stage, sector, check size
- **data_room**: Prepare due diligence materials
- **term_sheet**: Analyze and negotiate term sheets

## Investor Database

- 1,000+ investors tracked
- Angels, VCs, accelerators, family offices
- Stage: Seed through Series C
- Check size: $10k - $10M+

## Deliverables

- Pitch deck (10-15 slides)
- Investor shortlist (10-20)
- Data room template
- Term sheet analysis

## Pricing

- 5% of funds raised (success fee)
- Or $2,500/month advisory

## SafetyNet

- Raise > $500k → human review
- Valuation > $10M → compliance check

- Capital Swarm (primary)
