# Pillar 6: Tax Optimization

**Category**: finance
**Description**: Structure recommendations, treaty network analysis, tax filing support.

## Capabilities

- **structure_tax**: Recommend entity structure for tax efficiency
- **treaty_analyze**: Analyze tax treaties, identify benefits
- **filing_support**: Coordinate tax filings across jurisdictions
- **optimization**: Ongoing tax optimization recommendations

## Tax Treaties Database

- 3,000+ bilateral treaties tracked
- Withholding rates for dividends, interest, royalties
- Limitation of benefits articles
- Business income taxation rules

## Deliverables

- Tax structure recommendation
- Treaty benefit analysis
- Filing calendar
- Estimated tax liability

## SafetyNet

- Tax liability > $100k → human review
- Structure change → log to audit
- CRS/FATCA implications → flag
