# Pillar 1: Ideation - From Idea to Launch

**Category**: business
**Description**: Validating business ideas, market research, competitor analysis, and go-to-market strategy.

## Capabilities

- **idea_validation**: Assess business idea feasibility, market size, competitive landscape
- **market_research**: Identify target market, customer segments, market trends
- **competitor_analysis**: Analyze competitors, positioning, gaps, opportunities
- **gtm_strategy**: Develop go-to-market plan, channel strategy, pricing model

## Workflow

1. **Discovery**: Understand founder's idea, vision, constraints
2. **Research**: Market analysis, competitor mapping, customer interviews
3. **Validation**: Feasibility assessment, risk identification, opportunity sizing
4. **Strategy**: GTM plan, revenue model, growth strategy

## Deliverables

- Business canvas (validated)
- Market analysis report
- Competitor landscape map
- GTM strategy document

## Agents Involved

- Central Swarm (coordination)
- Growth Swarm (GTM, market)
- Strategy Agent (validation)

## Example

"Validate my SaaS idea for AI-powered accounting for digital nomads"

→ Research: $4.7B accounting software market, 31% YoY nomad growth
→ Competitors: QuickBooks, Xero, Bench (gaps in nomad-specific)
→ Validation: Strong market fit, recommend first-mover advantage
→ GTM: Content-first, partner with nomad communities
